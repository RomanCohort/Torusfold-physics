=== IMPORTANT CORRECTION to the earlier claim ===
The pre-fix guide force did NOT simply "repel" far pairs: its force sign was
opposite to its own potential gradient, so it pulled pairs INWARD at almost
every distance — all the way to overlap (~0.18 nm, where sigmoid saturates)
when other terms were absent. With the unbounded potential
E=-k*softplus((r-r0)/w), the correct gradient would push outward, but the
implemented force pulled inward regardless. Net effect in the real pipeline:
incoherent guide behaviour (energy says one thing, force does another), plus
no working range beyond ~r0.

The fix (450605d) makes guide = bounded near-attraction:
E=-k*softplus((r0-r)/w), F=-k/w*sig*delta/r. Verified: force matches the
potential gradient (direction checks PASS, isolated-term FD matches), it no
longer collapses pairs beyond r0, and it is inert beyond ~1.6 nm.

=== What this means for far-pair closure ===
The sign/potential bug is real and fixed, but closing pairs at >2 nm needs
more than the current guides: either a longer-range steering term, stronger
K, or the RL/steering path that operates before CG relaxation. Re-run the
2OIU Level-2 job (or a real segment) with 450605d and compare the far-pair
satisfaction metric; expect improvements only for pairs already within
~1.5 nm of pairing geometry.
