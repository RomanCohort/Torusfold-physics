KEY FINDINGS BEYOND THE ORIGINAL SIX:
A. The guide potential was pathological as written: E = -k*softplus((r-r0)/w)
   is unbounded below (r->inf stable), i.e. it pushed pairs apart no matter
   the force sign. Fixed to the bounded near-attraction form
   E = -k*softplus((r0-r)/w), force F = -k/w*sig*delta/r. All three
   implementations (unified, batched, cell-list) updated, energies AND
   forces (so REMD swap criteria now see the corrected surface too).
B. Working range of the guides is ~r0 + 3w ~ 1.6 nm; pairs beyond ~2 nm
   feel no guide force. If far-pair closure relies on these terms, the
   range itself is a design limit to revisit (consistent with far-pair
   closure difficulties).
C. The per-particle force caps (200 global / 50 GB) fire routinely for
   these terms (guide K/w up to 500 kJ/mol/nm), so F = -dE/dx is
   deliberately violated during most sampling steps. That is a stability
   choice; be aware REMD acceptance then samples a capped-force dynamics
   with uncapped swap energies.
