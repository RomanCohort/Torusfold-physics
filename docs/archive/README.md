# archive — superseded working record

Six documents that used to sit at the top of `docs/`. They are the *record of how the
numbers were arrived at*: the plans that were tried, the audits that were run, the
findings that came back. They are not deliverables -- the delivered reasoning is in
`docs/NOTES.md`, `docs/statistical_potentials_as_forces.md`, `docs/silent_defects.md`,
`docs/REPRODUCTION_RESOURCES.md` and `docs/attribution.md`.

**Why they are here rather than deleted.** Each is cited from a document that stays --
four of them by name in `docs/NOTES.md`'s index, `archive/pipeline_audit_2026-09-13.md` from
`docs/REPRODUCTION_RESOURCES.md`. Deleting them would have left a reader following a
dangling reference. Moving them costs one path prefix and no content.
